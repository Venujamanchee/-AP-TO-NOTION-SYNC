==================================================
        AP TO NOTION SYNC - CHROME EXTENSION
                     HOW TO USE GUIDE
==================================================

This extension is built for A/L Physics students to easily sync lesson video links and class details from ap.lk directly to their Notion databases with a single click.

Created by Venuja Manchee.

--------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO INSTALL (ADD TO YOUR BROWSER)
--------------------------------------------------
Since this is a custom extension, you can install it on Google Chrome (or Brave/Edge) in developer mode:

Step 1: Unzip the folder "AP-Notion-Sync" on your computer.
Step 2: Open Google Chrome and type "chrome://extensions" in the address bar, then press Enter.
Step 3: In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, turn ON the "Developer mode" switch.
Step 4: In the top-left corner, click the "Load unpacked" button.
Step 5: Select the unzipped folder containing these files.

The "AP to Notion Sync" icon (blue circle with custom logo) will appear in your Chrome toolbar!

--------------------------------------------------
2. HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------
* The extension runs 100% locally on your computer. It does not send your data to any cloud servers.
* When you open the extension while browsing ap.lk, a local scraper script reads the webpage elements on your screen (DOM Scraping) to extract the class topic, date, and unlisted YouTube link.
* When you click "Sync", the extension talks directly from your browser to Notion's servers to add the class details to your database.

--------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO SET UP (FIRST TIME ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------
Before syncing, you need to connect your Notion database:

Step 1: Go to https://www.notion.so/my-integrations and click "+ New integration".
Step 2: Name it (e.g., "Physics Class Sync") and submit. Copy the "Internal Integration Token".
Step 3: Open your Notion page (e.g. Physics-Anurada-Sir) in your browser.
Step 4: Click the three dots (•••) in the top-right corner of the page, go to "Connections" -> "Connect to", and select your integration.
Step 5: Copy the Page/Database ID (the 32-character code at the end of your Notion page URL).
Step 6: Open the extension popup, click the "Settings" tab, paste the Token and Page ID, select Target Type (Sub-page or Database), and click "Save Credentials".

--------------------------------------------------
4. NOTION DATABASE CONFIGURATION
--------------------------------------------------
If syncing to a Database table, ensure your database contains the following column headers exactly:

- "Unit"   -> Property Type: Title (This holds the class topic/name)
- "Date"   -> Property Type: Date  (Holds the parsed YYYY-MM-DD date)
- "Status" -> Property Type: Status (Will automatically fill as "Not started")
- "Select" -> Property Type: Select (Will automatically fill as "Theory")
- "Link"   -> Property Type: Text   (Holds the YouTube video link)

--------------------------------------------------
5. HOW TO SYNC A CLASS
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Open your lesson pack page on ap.lk and open a video.
Step 2: Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
Step 3: Review the parsed Topic, Date, and Link. The extension will automatically match the Unit.
Step 4: Click the "Sync to Notion" button.
Step 5: A checkmark toast will show "Synced successfully!". Check your Notion database, your video details will be saved!

How to set up? ▼
  1. Go to Notion Integrations and click + New integration.
  2. Give it a name (e.g. "Physics Class Sync") and submit. Copy the Internal Integration Token.
  3. Open your Notion page `Physics-Anurada-Sir`, click the three dots ••• in the top right, go to Connections -> Connect to, and select your integration.
  4. Copy your page ID from the URL (the 32-character code at the end of the URL).
  5. Paste the token and target ID above, select Sub-page, and click Save!
